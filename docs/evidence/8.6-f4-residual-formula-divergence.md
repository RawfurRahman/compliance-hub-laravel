# Residual Risk Formula Divergence (Path A vs Path B)

> Generated by throwaway comparison script. Intended to remain as documentation of
> why `ScoringEngine::calculateResidualInputs()` (Path A) is being consolidated onto
> `ResidualRiskService` (Path B) as the single authoritative residual implementation.

## Formulas

**Path A — legacy (ScoringEngine::calculateResidualInputs + residual_tv × residual_lh):**

    residual_tv = max(1, ceil(tv  × (1 - effectiveness/100)))
    residual_lh = max(1, ceil(lh  × (1 - effectiveness/100)))
    residual    = residual_tv × residual_lh

Notes: operates on the tv and likelihood axes only; the vulnerability factor is
absent from the residual product. Even at 0% effectiveness the result is tv×lh, not the
inherent score (unless vuln == 1).

**Path B — authoritative (ResidualRiskService, v1 weights):**

    control  = effectiveness/100
    reduction = control × 0.50 (control weight)
    residual = round(inherent × (1 - reduction))   [clamped 0..1, floor 1, never > inherent]

Notes: applies the weighted reduction to the full inherent score; can also include
treatment (×0.25), open-remediation inflation (×0.15), third-party (×0.10) and
low-evidence inflation (×0.15). For this grid only the control factor is varied and the
others are at neutral defaults (treatment 0, no open remediation, not waived, no
third-party dependence, evidence confidence 100).

## Observations

- Path A collapses to tv × lh regardless of vulnerability; Path B reduces the full inherent score.
- At 0% effectiveness Path B returns the inherent score; Path A returns tv × lh (1-30x lower).
- At 100% effectiveness Path A floors to 1 (min residual_tv/residual_lh); Path B retains 50% of
  the inherent score (control weight 0.50).
- The two models can only agree when vuln == 1 AND effectiveness == 0 (both yield tv × lh).

Divergence at every non-trivial grid point is the justification for consolidating onto
Path B (versioned, weighted, reproducible, waiver-aware).
