# Residual Risk Formula Divergence (Path A vs Path B)

> Generated by throwaway comparison script. Intended to remain as documentation of
> why `ScoringEngine::calculateResidualInputs()` (Path A) is being consolidated onto
> `ResidualRiskService` (Path B) as the single authoritative residual implementation.

## Formulas

**Path A — legacy (ScoringEngine::calculateResidualInputs + residual_tv × residual_lh):**

    residual_tv = max(1, ceil(tv  × (1 - effectiveness/100)))
    residual_lh = max(1, ceil(lh  × (1 - effectiveness/100)))
    residual    = residual_tv × residual_lh

Notes: operates on the tv and likelihood axes only; the vulnerability factor is
absent from the residual product. Even at 0% effectiveness the result is tv×lh, not the
inherent score (unless vuln == 1).

**Path B — authoritative (ResidualRiskService, v1 weights):**

    control  = effectiveness/100
    reduction = control × 0.50 (control weight)
    residual = round(inherent × (1 - reduction))   [clamped 0..1, floor 1, never > inherent]

Notes: applies the weighted reduction to the full inherent score; can also include
treatment (×0.25), open-remediation inflation (×0.15), third-party (×0.10) and
low-evidence inflation (×0.15). For this grid only the control factor is varied and the
others are at neutral defaults (treatment 0, no open remediation, not waived, no
third-party dependence, evidence confidence 100).

## Observations

- Path A collapses to tv × lh regardless of vulnerability; Path B reduces the full inherent score.
- At 0% effectiveness Path B returns the inherent score; Path A returns tv × lh (1-30x lower).
- At 100% effectiveness Path A floors to 1 (min residual_tv/residual_lh); Path B retains 50% of
  the inherent score (control weight 0.50).
- The two models can only agree when vuln == 1 AND effectiveness == 0 (both yield tv × lh).

Divergence at every non-trivial grid point is the justification for consolidating onto
Path B (versioned, weighted, reproducible, waiver-aware).

## The single authoritative formula (plain words)

> Residual risk is the inherent risk that remains after control and treatment effort
> reduces it. It is computed as follows:
>
> 1. Take the inherent score (threat x vulnerability x likelihood product).
> 2. Compute the effective reduction, which starts at the weighted contribution of the
>    mapped controls (control effectiveness, capped at 100%, times weight 0.50) plus the
>    weighted contribution of treatment plans (average effectiveness x progress, times
>    weight 0.25).
> 3. Subtract inflation penalties: +0.15 if there is an open remediation item, +0.10 if
>    the risk depends on a third party, +0.15 if evidence confidence is low.
> 4. Clamp the resulting effective reduction to the range 0..1.
> 5. The residual score is `round(inherent x (1 - effective reduction))`, floored at 1 when
>    the inherent score is non-zero, and never allowed to exceed the inherent score.
> 6. A waived risk (approved risk acceptance, or lifecycle status `accepted`) receives
>    ZERO reduction — it is documented, not reduced — regardless of controls or treatment.
>
> Every residual score persisted to the database (computed_residual_rating,
> risk_scores_history.residual_rating, risk_residual_scores.residual_score) flows through
> this single implementation: `ResidualRiskService`. There is no second residual formula.

## Decision: pre-existing stored residual scores (recorded 2026-08-17)

- Historical rows in `risk_scores_history` and `risk_residual_scores` are append-only audit
  data. They are KEPT as-is and now represent scores computed under the legacy formula.
- Live `computed_residual_rating` values on risks created BEFORE this consolidation were
  computed under the legacy formula (residual_tv x residual_lh, no vulnerability factor,
  no weighted reduction). They are therefore INCONSISTENT with the current authoritative
  formula and are annotated as legacy values.
- DECISION: no automatic, silent rewrite of stored data. Existing values are annotated.
  A one-off backfill (run `RiskService::recalculateRisk` per risk) is the sanctioned way to
  normalize legacy rows and is available on request.
- Fresh recomputations (any risk save / control map / unmap / import / recalc trigger) use
  the authoritative formula immediately.
